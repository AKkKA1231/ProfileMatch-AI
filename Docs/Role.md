# The Recipe — Prompting Framework

## R — Role
**Tell the AI WHO it should pretend to be.**
> *"You are an expert Python tutor."*

## T — Task
**Tell the AI exactly WHAT to do.**
> *"Explain how a for-loop works."*

## F — Format
**Tell the AI HOW you want the answer.**
> *"Answer in 3 short bullet points with one example."*

---
*Use these every time you really care about the answer.*
