Job Title: Senior Technical Product Manager - AI & Data Products
Location: Boston, MA (Hybrid)
Experience Required: 5+ years

Role Overview:
We are looking for a Senior Technical Product Manager to define the strategy, roadmap, and execution for AI-powered SaaS products. You will bridge technical engineering teams with business stakeholders to ship intuitive, context-aware AI tools.

Key Responsibilities:
- Define product vision, customer personas, roadmap prioritization, and feature specs for AI/ML capabilities.
- Translate business objectives into detailed technical user stories, acceptance criteria, and API requirements.
- Analyze product metrics (DAU, retention, NPS, latency, conversion) to drive iterative feature improvements.
- Collaborate closely with ML engineers, UX designers, and executive leaders in an Agile framework.

Required Qualifications & Skills:
- 5+ years of product management experience shipping B2B SaaS or AI-driven enterprise products.
- Technical background with a strong understanding of Machine Learning, APIs, and data analytics workflows.
- Proven track record of user research, wireframing, A/B testing, and data-informed decision making.
- Outstanding verbal and written communication skills with strong executive presentation ability.
- MBA or B.S. in Computer Science, Engineering, or technical discipline.
